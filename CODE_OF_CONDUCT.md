# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want CareBridge to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to software or healthcare.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for patients, clinicians and the community.
- Protect privacy: never share real patient information or other people's personal details.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory jokes and comments
- Sexualised language or unwelcome attention
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Sharing real patient data in any project space
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they judge harmful.

Report problems privately to **conduct@YOUR-DOMAIN** (replace with a real address before publishing). Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers will not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when someone represents the project in public.
